How it plays

Tap a tile and every connected tile of that color burns.

The wind carries the fire one step further — and whatever it lands on burns too.

Sparks are limited. Pick the cluster with the best neighbors downwind, not the biggest one.

Every level you clear relights a hearth in Wintermere, a snowed-in mountain village, and brings another family home.
