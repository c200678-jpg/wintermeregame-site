Before you play

Welcome back. This is a second visit with the same invitation.

This is an early prototype of a puzzle game. We are testing the game, not you, and there is no right way to play.

  • It takes about 15 minutes at most. The test ends by itself when the time is up.
  • The game records your moves, their timing, your screen size and your answers to five short questions at the end. It does not record your name, email, contacts or location. Please don't type personal details in the answers. (As with any website, the hosting services see your connection's IP address; the game does not store it.)
  • Results go to the developer's private spreadsheet under a random code, are kept for up to 90 days for this test, and are then deleted. To have yours deleted sooner, email ramovicdigital.support@gmail.com with the code from your invitation.
  • You can stop at any time: tap ← and "I'm done", or just close the page.
  • No account, no purchases, no ads.
